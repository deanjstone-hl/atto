A collector run

Every 30 minutes a homelab timer reads the car and posts one reading. State is saved only after the Worker accepts it.

Sources: docs/byd-collector.md, collector/, worker/ingest.ts, ADR-006. The reader calls get_vehicles and get_vehicle_realtime and nothing else; it runs on the second BYD account, never the owner's main one. Secrets are read by name from Secrets Manager. Nothing runs it until the owner turns it on. [speculation] Whether the timer is live now was not checked.
